## 2. #93 — HUMAN FORCED-CHOICE. THE ONLY RESEARCH ITEM LEFT.

**Blocker: a 1080p projector.** The old one died 2026-08-01. There is no software substitute, and
that is not an assumption — it was tested and failed for a principled reason (**E.10.2**): *every
damage measure this project owns confounds content with damage.* Banding and blocking because
complexity MASKS them; perPoint because complexity SUPPLIES the detail. Combining contaminated
measures yields a third contaminated measure.

### Arm 1 — the JND anchor (where does Brennan call a film "perfect"?)

`scripts/jnd-table.js` gives the scale a unit, but its **link 3** (1 JND ≈ 2.5 CRF) is BORROWED, and
its own header calls it *"the weak link"*. **This week measured the analogous VMAF constant and found
the borrowed value 46% too large** (16.42 assumed, 11.28 measured, `data/vmaf-ladder.json` n=41). So
borrowed constants in this chain have a track record. Arm 1 replaces a guessed origin with a chosen
one; it is a single offset, not a scale.

### Arm 2 — P's validity. **This is the sharper one and the bigger risk.**

**P has never been checked against anything human.** Everything known about it is internal
consistency: it reproduces (reliability 0.674 at 4 clips), its structure is 3+1 (λ₁ = 1.399), its size
is measured (provShare 0.1089), and it passes its pre-registered direction check (96/20000 against a
bar of 100). **None of that establishes that the reproducible thing is PROVENANCE rather than a
mixture.**

**The sharpest form of the problem:** the ADEQUACY blend is **banding alone** (banding 90.9%, grain
4.9%, blur 0.3%, blocking −4.0% — §9.0h). So three of the four detectors justify their existence
**only** through P. **If P does not hold up, we are measuring four things to use one.**

**Blur is the most exposed.** §9.0j kept it in P — the joint statistic favours it — but *only via the
WEB-vs-Bluray contrast, which is bits-dominated*. Blur **dilutes** the encoder-identity contrast
(adj R² peaks at w≈0 and falls to 0.109 by w=1). That is a materially weaker footing than "all four
load", and it makes blur the detector most likely to be overturned here.

## 6. THE DISCIPLINE THAT MATTERS MORE THAN ANY RESULT

Full list: `docs/BPP-PLUS.txt` §10, traps 1–29. **The two that earned their place this week:**

**BINNING MANUFACTURES STRUCTURE.** Twice in one night a binned view showed a clean, significant
effect that the continuous form refused: the grain stratification (t = 2.42 → interaction t = −0.08)
and operating-point dependence (monotone 3/3 → interaction t = −0.47). **Binning does not merely lose
power; it creates apparent structure.** The continuous test is the arbiter every time.

**PRECISION IS NOT AUTHORITY.** A biased estimator becoming precise is *worse* than one staying
vague. The discredited pooled derivative's CI stopped including 1 exactly as it got more wrong. **A
consistent estimator converges as n grows; a biased one drifts while its CI tightens** — watching that
happen was stronger evidence than any single test.

**A PRE-REGISTERED RULE IS ONLY AS GOOD AS ITS STATISTIC** (trap 30, learned the hard way on the last
task of the session). #107 registered "GT grain range ≥ 2.0× reopens the grain leg" honestly and in
advance, measured 2.76×, and said REOPEN — **wrongly**. `max/min` is an extreme-value statistic:
dropping one clip took it to 1.81×, and the spread that actually drives an interaction's SE (p90/p10)
was 1.47× against the proxy's 1.42× — barely moved. The substantive test agreed and the original
answer stood. **Register on a robust quantity that DRIVES the decision — sd, or an interquantile
spread. Ask: "how many single observations could flip this?"**

**DO NOT CLOSE A TASK ON PARTIAL DATA FROM THE RUN THAT WOULD DECIDE IT** (trap 31). #73 was closed
citing "at 24/41 the range is 1.72×"; the completed run read 2.76× and the closure was reversed eight
minutes later, then restored by the real test. **Right answer, reached twice by faulty routes — that
is luck, not method.** If a running job bears on a decision, the decision waits for the job.

**And the standing rule that caught the most:** check a number against an **external bound** before
its standard error (trap 8). It fired on the >1 exponents before any diagnosis existed.

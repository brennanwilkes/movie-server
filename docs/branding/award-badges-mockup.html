Cannes + Sundance badge proposals

How festival wins would sit next to the existing Oscar badges on posters, wide previews and the compact card form — web client and Fire Stick fork. The Oscar rendering on every panel is the exact production CSS/params; the film-reel (Cannes) and sun (Sundance) glyphs are locked. Design rules: one row per festival (Cannes and Sundance are separate lines, just like Oscar wins/noms are), a single win shows the award name, and the 4-line stack never happens so it isn't shown.

Design notes · Festival wins are winners-only (there's no festival "nominations" dataset), so the badge line is always a win/award — which is exactly why it stays to one line per festival. One row per festival (never combined); a single win shows the award name on the web plaque, and the Fire Stick compact form is always glyph + count. The 4-line stack never occurs (0 of 2,213 films), so it's omitted. Tag proposal: festival-cannes-N / festival-sundance-N (per-film counts derived from oscar-winners.json), written by a tmdb-keyed pass in oscar-tags.js; both clients widen their regex and render the extra row. Laurel #9fb87f + amber #f26d3d are the proposed festival palette (amber reuses Canyon's accent2 so it feels native). Glyphs: film reel (Cannes) + sun (Sundance) are chosen; palm, clapper, mountain+sun and snowflake were the rejected alternatives. Web pill note: today's pill shows losing noms while the plaque shows total — the mockup preserves that existing behaviour, it's not new.
